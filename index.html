Methodology & Source Ledger

This dashboard exists to verify a downloaded "AI research" document proposing 30 project-controls methods and a 4-tier dashboard architecture for mission-critical, multi-site manufacturing programs — via 7 independent research passes, each requiring ≥2 corroborating sources and a disconfirming search before accepting a claim. Verified 2026-09-05.

A cross-cutting pattern: borrowed regulated-industry vocabulary

The single most important finding of this verification pass: three independent research agents, working on unrelated sections of the document, each found the same class of error — specific compliance vocabulary borrowed from one regulated industry and applied broadly to "mission-critical manufacturing" without caveat. APQP/PPAP is AIAG-automotive-native (18 elements); aerospace uses the distinct AS9145 standard (11 deliverables) — not raw PPAP. IQ/OQ/PQ is FDA/pharma-GxP-native (21 CFR §211.63); even semiconductor-industry guides admit borrowing "the pharmaceutical industry's 3Q framework." Common Data Environment (CDE) is construction/BIM-native (ISO 19650) — no evidence of genuine manufacturing/PLM usage was found; the real manufacturing-native term is "digital thread" or "unified data environment." Each is corrected on its own tab, not silently repeated here.

Confirmed corrections (not silently repeated as fact)

  • CORRECTED "CBS" is not a real EVMS pillar term — the actual fourth structural element alongside WBS/OBS is the Chart of Accounts (NDIA/EIA-748 Intent Guide).
  • CORRECTED "EBOM → MBOM → PBOM" is not a real 3-tier manufacturing lineage — Planning BOM (PBOM) is an MRP demand-planning construct ("super bill"), not a manufacturing-execution tier after MBOM.
  • CORRECTED "WIP Value-at-Risk" is not an established named practice — a plausible-sounding document-authored synthesis of three separately-real concepts (VaR, WIP aging, scrap risk).
  • CORRECTED "Vendor Performance Indexing (VPI)" is not a standardized named metric — it appears as an unrelated proprietary term in property-management vendor scoring, confirming it is reinvented per-organization, not an industry standard. The correctly-named metrics are OTIF %, Defect PPM, and Billing Variance %.
  • CORRECTED RPN (Severity × Occurrence × Detection) was replaced by Action Priority (AP) in the joint AIAG-VDA FMEA Handbook (2019) specifically because RPN's multiplication can rate a high-severity failure as low-priority — this dashboard's FMEA tab uses AP, not RPN alone.
  • CORRECTED The document's own dashboard "Level 1-4" numbering collides with (but does not match) both ISA-95's real Level 0-4 automation hierarchy and ISA-101's separate Level 1-4 HMI hierarchy — three different schemes share the same numbers in the same domain. This dashboard's tab names deliberately avoid "Level 1-4" language for this reason.
  • CORRECTED "Under three clicks" drill-down is the specific, 20+-year-old debunked UX myth (Porter 2003: no drop-off in success/satisfaction after 3 clicks, users click 25+ times without friction when they feel they're making progress; Nielsen: changing a design from 3 to 4 clicks increased conversion 600%). Not used as a design requirement here.

Confirmed real, with a materiality caveat

  • REAL, contested: "Zero tolerance for negative float" is real DCMA-audit-threshold language (DCMA Check #7), but is contested in practitioner literature as a rigid policy that can mask root causes in complex schedules rather than fix them.
  • REAL, optimistic: "Daily/automated end-of-day EVMS" refresh is not standard practice at multi-billion-dollar program scale — real ANSI/EIA-748 cadence is monthly; cost-accrual lag is the specific structural reason EVM cannot be genuinely daily. This independently reconfirms, in a different domain, an identical finding from an earlier manufacturing-cost-KPI research pass this session.
  • REAL, thin evidence for the broader grouping: multi-site tooling/equipment leveling is strongly evidenced for autoclaves specifically (real, peer-reviewed aerospace-composite scheduling literature — genuine $1-5M capital bottleneck economics) but thin for grouping CNCs/metrology rigs/calibration chambers into the same claim.
  • REAL, often over-claimed: "digital twin" multi-tier supply-chain visibility has real leading-edge case studies (Ford, JD.com) but a 2024 peer-reviewed study found only ~16% of self-labeled "digital twins" achieve true closed-loop capability — most are rebranded monitoring dashboards.
